# UCA — United Cooperative Assurance

## Status

**Licensed for medical. No provider list published. The live website is unreachable.**

UCA's product pages describe a **Medical Insurance** line, and its company page states it is
licensed by the Insurance Authority for "Property, Casualty and Accident, Liability, **Medical**,
Marine Cargo and Hull, Aviation, Engineering and Energy Insurances."

But it publishes no provider list and no network lookup. **No hospital may be named from this
skill.**

**Important caveat on sourcing:** `uca.com.sa` **does not respond** — every connection times out.
Everything below comes from **Internet Archive snapshots** of UCA's own pages, the newest being
**14 February 2025**. The content is UCA's; the vintage is the archive's.

Verified 11 November 2026. See `sources/index.md`.

## Do not confuse UCA with ACIG

Both have Arabic names beginning المتحدة, and both are Saudi insurers. They are **different
companies**:

| | **UCA** | **ACIG** |
|---|---|---|
| English | United Cooperative Assurance | United Cooperative Assurance **Group** / ACIG |
| Arabic | الشركة المتحدة للتأمين التعاوني | المجموعة المتحدة للتأمين التعاوني (أسيج) |
| Domain | `uca.com.sa` | `acig.com.sa` |
| Established | 2008 (inherited a 35-year portfolio) | — |
| Member portal | No | **Toshfa** |

If a user says "United", ask which one.

## The rule this skill exists to protect

A hospital may accept UCA and still not accept the member's class. This skill never answers at
company level. It holds no provider names, so its only correct provider answer is
**"I cannot confirm this"**, plus the hospital's phone number and the exact question:

> *"Do you bill UCA Class [A/B] directly?"*

## What UCA publishes — verified from the archive

**United Cooperative Assurance Company (UCA)** — a Saudi joint stock company **established May
2008**, underwriting since January 2009, paid capital SAR 400 million. Head office Riyadh, branches
across the Kingdom.

### Licensed lines, in its own words

> "The services offered by the company covers all insurance lines that the company is licensed for
> by the Insurance Authority which includes Property, Casualty and Accident, Liability, **Medical**,
> Marine Cargo and Hull, Aviation, Engineering and Energy Insurances."

### The Medical product, in its own words

> "This insurance provides cover for the costs of providing Healthcare to the insured persons **to
> the extent and in the network of medical services providers appointed by the Company**."

That sentence is the important one: **UCA states that a network exists and that the Company appoints
it.** It then publishes nothing about who is in it.

The product description goes on to list benefits — consultations, diagnosis, treatment, medicine,
hospitalisation, surgeries, day surgeries, obstetrics and delivery, dental and gum disease,
preventive measures and vaccinations, maternity and child care, acute psychological disorders,
contagious disease isolation, and repatriation of the deceased — all "as shown in the policy
schedule."

**"As shown in the policy schedule"** is the tell: the benefits and the network live on the
member's own schedule, not on the website.

## What is absent

| Aspect | Published? |
|---|---|
| Provider list (any format) | **No** |
| Public provider/network lookup | **No** |
| City-level network | **No** |
| Tier/class names | **No** |
| Class A / Class B mapping | **No** |

This is the SAICO and Salama pattern a third time: the insurer names the mechanism (an appointed
network) and withholds the contents.

## What the agent must do for a UCA user

1. Confirm the company is **UCA / United Cooperative Assurance** (الشركة المتحدة للتأمين التعاوني)
   and **not ACIG** (المجموعة المتحدة / أسيج). If unsure, ask.
2. Ask the class on their card (Class A / Class B). If they do not know, say where to look and wait.
3. Ask the city and the service.
4. **Do not name a hospital.** Say plainly that UCA does not publish its appointed provider network —
   it states the network exists but not who is in it.
5. Give them what does work:
   - **Call UCA: 800 244 4442.**
   - **Check the policy schedule** — UCA's own product text says benefits and network are "as shown
     in the policy schedule", so the member's document may name the network.
   - **Ask the hospital directly:** *"Do you bill UCA Class [A/B] directly?"*
6. State that coverage and networks change, so confirm before travelling.
7. **If the user needs current information and the phone line fails**, note that UCA's website has
   been unreachable — they may need the Insurance Authority's register or their employer's HR to
   establish the current position.
