# Arabian Shield — plans, classes, and what is publicly known

Populated from Arabian Shield's own site (`der3.com`, English and Arabic), read 11 November
2026.

## The health product line — one product

Arabian Shield's product menu offers health insurance, but describes only a single product:

| Product | Arabic name | Buyer |
|---|---|---|
| SME Medical Insurance | تأمين صحي — وثيقة التأمين الخاصة بالمنشآت/الشركات الصغيرة | Small & medium establishments |

The description, in full, is:

> "Reassurance on human and family health works as a stimulus for everyone to do more.
> Arabian Shield seeks to provide a healthy environment and guarantees the right of insured to
> receive full treatment and health care he needs through a wide and distinctive network of
> hospitals."

That is it. There is no benefits table, no room-type or deductible description, and no tier
list — all of which Bupa and MedGulf publish at least in outline.

## Other products on the menu (not health)

Motor, travel, personal accident, medical malpractice, artisans, home, commercial insurance.
The medical malpractice product is professional liability for practitioners — not a
treatment product for members — and its portal (`direct.der3.com/mmp`) is a claim/service
platform, not a directory.

## Classes and tiers — nothing published

| Aspect | Published by Arabian Shield? |
|---|---|
| Number of classes | **No** |
| Class names | **No** |
| Class A / Class B designation | **No** |
| Benefits per class | **No** |
| Class → provider mapping | **No** |
| Provider list | **No** |

Compare with the other companies checked:

- Bupa: names five levels and publishes a file per level.
- MedGulf: names five RIADI tiers with provider counts.
- Malath: uses the word "classes" once in marketing copy.
- **Arabian Shield: none of the above.** Its health page is a single paragraph.

This is the thinnest public disclosure of the five. Note it in the write-up as the bottom of
the range — and note that it is not the same kind of absence as Tawuniya's: Tawuniya at least
lets a member look up a specific hospital; Arabian Shield publishes no lookup either.

## Group vs individual

The menu route "تأمين الأفراد" (individuals) covers motor, travel, personal accident,
malpractice, artisans and home — **not** health. So Arabian Shield's health offering appears
targeted at establishments rather than individuals. Do not tell a user they can buy individual
health insurance from Arabian Shield on the basis of this site.

## Implications for the skill

Because there is nothing to place a member in — no tier, no class name, no list — the agent's
only honest move is to acknowledge the company, explain that its network is not published, and
give the telephone route. That is exactly what `SKILL.md` instructs.
