# Wataniya — products, and the health-product absence

Populated from Wataniya's own site (`wataniya.com.sa`), read 11 November 2026.

## Who they are

**الشركة الوطنية للتأمين** — The National Insurance Company, trading as **Wataniya**. Jeddah-based.
Site title: *"الشركة الوطنية للتأمين أون لاين جدة، المملكة العربية السعودية"*.

**Not Tawuniya.** Tawuniya is **التعاونية**, Riyadh, `tawuniya.com.sa` — a different company that
does sell health. The English names differ by one letter and are commonly confused.

## The malpractice distinction — the one place "medical" appears

Wataniya's medical-adjacent product is **Medical Malpractice**:

> "إذا كنت ممارسًا طبيًا يوفر لك هذا التـأمين حماية قانونية متوافقة مع أحكام الشريعة الإسلامية
> تحميك من أي شكاوى أو اخطاء تتعلق بالسهو أثناء مزاولة المهنة"

Translated: *"If you are a medical practitioner, this insurance provides Sharia-compliant legal
protection against any complaints or errors relating to negligence while practising the
profession."*

**This is professional indemnity for a doctor.** It pays if a practitioner is sued. It is not
treatment cover, it has no member, no hospital and no network, and it cannot be used at a
reception desk.

If a user is a practitioner with a Wataniya malpractice policy and asks about hospitals, the
question does not apply to their policy at all — their patients' cover is a separate matter.

## Why this entry exists in the set

Most companies in this project were checked for *what they publish* about health. Wataniya is the
first checked where the answer is **the product does not exist**. That is a different kind of
finding and belongs in the write-up:

- Twelve insurers publish nothing useful about their health network.
- **Wataniya publishes nothing about health because it sells none.**

Recording it prevents a future reader assuming the check was skipped, and it catches the
Tawuniya/Wataniya confusion that would otherwise send a user to the wrong company entirely.
