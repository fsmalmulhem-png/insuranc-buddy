# Gulf Union — products, classes, and what is published

Populated from Gulf Union's e-services portal (`eservices.gulfunion.com.sa`) and its application
bundle, read 11 November 2026.

## Who they are

**الاتحاد الخليجي للتأمين** — Gulf Union Cooperative Insurance. Not to be confused with
**الخليجية العامة** (Gulf General / GGCI), a different Saudi insurer with its own skill.

The public domain `gulfunion.com.sa` and `www.gulfunion.com.sa` both redirect to
**`eservices.gulfunion.com.sa`**, an Angular customer portal. There is no separate corporate
marketing site reachable from that domain.

## Health products

| Product | Gulf Union's own line |
|---|---|
| **Health Insurance** | "Covers Medical Practices in Terms of Consultation and More" |
| **Medical SME** | "For Small & Medium Establishments to Be Perfect Health Insurance" |
| **Medical Malpractice** | "Protects Healthcare Practitioners From the Liabilities of Medical Errors" |

Other lines on the same product menu: Motor, Home, Marine, Engineering, Domestic, Savings.

### Observation worth recording

The product listing puts **health under corporate/SME** framing, and the only individual-facing
medical product named is **Medical Malpractice** — which is liability insurance for
practitioners, not treatment cover for a member. So an individual holding Gulf Union health cover
most likely has it through an employer.

Do not tell a user they can buy individual health from Gulf Union on the basis of this site.

## Classes and tiers

| Aspect | Published? |
|---|---|
| Tier names (VIP / Gold / Silver) | **No** |
| Class A / Class B | **No** |
| Benefits per tier | **No** |
| Class → provider mapping | **No** |
| Provider list | **No** — member session only |

The application bundle contains **no class vocabulary**. Searches for `VIP`, `Gold`, `Silver`,
`Bronze` and tier-like labels returned nothing relevant; the only "category" fields are product
categories and profession categories used in claim submission forms.

So Gulf Union is in the majority group of this set: no published class structure.
