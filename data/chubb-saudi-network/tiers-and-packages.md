## The two near-misses, and why neither is health

### Travel Insurance

Chubb Arabia does sell travel insurance — and travel policies commonly include **emergency medical
cover while abroad**.

That is **not** domestic health insurance:

| Travel medical | Health insurance |
|---|---|
| Emergency treatment during a trip | Routine and elective treatment |
| Time-limited by the trip | Continuous while the policy runs |
| Usually excludes pre-existing conditions | Covers ongoing conditions to a degree |
| No hospital network relationship at home | Network-based |

A member cannot use a Chubb travel policy for a check-up in Dammam. This is the single most likely
confusion and the skill calls it out by name.

### Group Life

Listed in the About page's portfolio summary. **Group life pays a benefit on death** — it is not a
treatment benefit, has no hospital network, and cannot be presented at a reception desk.

Group life is often bundled with group medical at other insurers, which is exactly why someone
might assume Chubb's group life comes with health. Here it does not.

### Personal Accident

Also listed. Pays for accidental injury or death. It is not routine care, and it does not make a
hospital a network provider.

## The global-brand trap — worth recording

Chubb is a large global insurer and **does** sell health products in other markets. A user who has
held Chubb health elsewhere may reasonably assume it exists in Saudi Arabia.

It does not. The Saudi entity is a P&C carrier. This is the same trap as Wataniya/Tawuniya, in a
different form: the brand is real and well known, but the product does not exist in this market.

**The skill must not let brand familiarity substitute for checking the product.**
