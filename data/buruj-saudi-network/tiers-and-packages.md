# Buruj — what transferred, and what stopped

Populated from MEDGULF's own merger FAQ (`medgulf.com.sa/home/buruj-faqs`), read 11 November 2026.
Buruj's own site is unreachable, so MEDGULF is the authoritative source for what happened.

## The merger

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Parties | **Buruj Cooperative Insurance** (بروج) + **MEDGULF** (المتوسط والخليج) |
| Completed | **26 October 2025** |
| Result | Single entity under the **MEDGULF** name |
| Branches | All Buruj branches become MEDGULF branches |

## What continues — MEDGULF's assurances

- **Policies continue unchanged.** "All your current services and policies, as well as support
  channels, will continue as usual without any changes."
- **Terms unchanged.** "There will be no changes to the insurance terms, and they will remain
  fully compliant with the requirements of the Insurance Authority."
- **Branches keep serving Buruj customers.**

## What stopped

| Stopped | Replacement |
|---|---|
| **Policy issuance through Buruj** | Renew through MEDGULF |
| Buruj's website | Nothing — the domain does not respond |
| Buruj as a brand | MEDGULF |

MEDGULF's wording: *"The issuance of policies through Buruj has been stopped. If your policy
expires, you can renew it through MEDGULF."*

## The two-level service split — important

MEDGULF's FAQ distinguishes **policy level** from **member level**:

| Level | Motor / General / **Medical policy** | Member level |
|---|---|---|
| Who | 8004414442 | **(TCS) 920014001** |
| Hours | Sun–Thu, 8:00–16:00 | **24/7** |

So a Buruj member's medical questions — approvals, claims, network — go to **TCS at 920014001**,
not to the MEDGULF toll-free number. That distinction is easy to miss and it is the one that
matters for a medical query.

## The TCS connection

TCS also appears in this project's **Al Sagr** skill, as the administrator of Al Sagr's medical
network (8003037858 / 920012493). Here TCS serves Buruj-turned-MEDGULF members at **920014001**.

This is worth recording in the write-up for one reason: **a member's real answer may come from a
company whose name appears nowhere on their card.** The card says Buruj (now MEDGULF); the answer
comes from TCS.

## The app caveat

> "Currently the MEDGULF app does not support Buruj policies."

A Buruj member whose policy does not appear in the MEDGULF app has not lost their policy. They
should use the TCS line. This is worth saying explicitly because the natural interpretation —
"my policy is gone" — is wrong and alarming.

## Classes

Buruj's classes are not published anywhere now. The merger preserved policy terms, so a Buruj
member's class is whatever their policy schedule says — but MEDGULF publishes no class-to-provider
mapping either (see the `medgulf-saudi-network` skill), so the answer still has to come from TCS or
the hospital.

## Where Buruj sits

Buruj is unique in this set: it is the only company included that **no longer exists as an
independent insurer**. Every other skill documents a live company's publication behaviour. This one
documents a merger and redirects.
