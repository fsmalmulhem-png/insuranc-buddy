# Aljazira Takaful — products and the TCS relationship

Populated from Aljazira Takaful's own site (`aljaziratakaful.com.sa`), read 11 November 2026.

## Who they are

**Aljazira Takaful Taawuni** — الجزيرة تكافل. Takaful (cooperative) insurance. The email domain is
`ajt.com.sa`, which is where the "AJT" abbreviation comes from — but `ajt.com.sa` itself no longer
serves a website.

## The medical products — three lines

| Product | Route | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual Medical** | `/IndividualProducts/Medical` | Individuals |
| **Corporate Medical** | `/CorporateProducts/Medical` | Companies and institutions, plus their families |
| **Medical Domestic Helper** | `/IndividualProducts/MedicalDomesticHelper` | Domestic workers |
| Medical Malpractice | `/IndividualProducts/MedicalMalpractice` | Practitioners (liability, not treatment) |

Three separate medical lines is broader than most companies in this set — several offer only
corporate/SME health, and only Wataniya offers none.

## The description, verbatim

> "Aljazira Takaful medical insurance policy provides comprehensive medical care to the employees
> in the companies and institutions and their family members, thus ensuring that you receive the
> best medical care through a **wide network of hospitals and medical centers across the kingdom**"

followed by the bullet: **"Wide Medical Network covering entire Kingdom."**

That is the whole of the network description — marketing language, no names, no cities, no tiers.

## The TCS relationship — stated by the insurer itself

This is the most valuable finding for this company. Aljazira Takaful's own pages say:

> **"Medical Services available 24/7"**
>
> "To access medical services, please call the unified number **8003040400 and select extension
> (2)**, or download the medical insurance app."

And the app it links to:

| Store | Link indicator |
|---|---|
| iOS | `apps.apple.com/sa/app/**total-care-saudi**/id1587870212` |
| Android | `play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=**com.chatilaah.tc**` |

The site's own media folder also contains **`Content/media/TCS_Footer.jpg`** — TCS branding placed
in Aljazira Takaful's footer.

**Reading:** Aljazira Takaful does not run its own medical network. TCS does, and the insurer
hands the member over explicitly — phone extension, app, and branding all pointing to TCS.

## The TCS group in this project

| Insurer | TCS route published |
|---|---|
| **Al Sagr** | 8003037858 / 920012493, 24/7 + portal `tcs-ksa.net:1192` |
| **Buruj** (→ MEDGULF) | 920014001, 24/7 |
| **Aljazira Takaful** | **8003040400 ext. 2, 24/7** + Total Care Saudi app |

Three separate insurers, one administrator, three different numbers — each published by the
insurer. This is now the strongest evidence in the project that the **network layer is
outsourced**, and that the name on the card is not the name that answers.
