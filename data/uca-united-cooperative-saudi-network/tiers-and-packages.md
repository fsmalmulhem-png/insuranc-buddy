# UCA — licensed lines, the medical product, and the class gap

Populated from archived snapshots of UCA's own site (`uca.com.sa`), read 11 November 2026. The live
site does not respond; see `sources/index.md`.

## The company

> "United Cooperative Assurance Company (UCA) is a Saudi joint stock company **established in May
> 2008**, where the company had started underwriting insurance business except Protection and
> Savings Insurance since **January 2009** with a paid Capital of **SAR 400 Million**."

> "UCA had inherited the portfolio and the management of its main shareholder UCA Insurance Company
> who was active for over thirty five years in servicing the Saudi market"

Head office **Riyadh**, with branches "in all the main areas of the Kingdom".

## Licensed lines

> "The services offered by the company covers all insurance lines that the company is licensed for
> by the Insurance Authority which includes **Property, Casualty and Accident, Liability, Medical,
> Marine Cargo and Hull, Aviation, Engineering and Energy Insurances**."

Medical is one of nine licensed lines. UCA is a general insurer with a medical offering, not a
health specialist.

## The Medical product

UCA's product page describes it as:

> "This insurance provides cover for the costs of providing Healthcare to the insured persons **to the
> extent and in the network of medical services providers appointed by the Company**."

**Two facts in one sentence:** a network exists, and UCA appoints it. Nothing about its contents.

### Recoverable expenses, as listed

| Category | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Health benefits** | Medical consultation, diagnosis, treatment and medicine — "as shown in the policy schedule" |
| **Hospitalisation** | Surgeries, same-day surgeries, obstetrics and delivery |
| **Dental** | Dental and gum disease treatment |
| **Preventive** | Vaccinations including seasonal, maternity and child care per Ministry of Health |
| **Psychological** | Acute psychological disorders "within the limits specified in the policy schedule" |
| **Contagious disease** | Isolation in hospitals as specified by the Ministry of Health |
| **Repatriation** | Preparation and repatriation of the corpse to home country per the labour contract |

Every quantitative limit is deferred to **"the policy schedule"**. That is where the member's class
and network live too.
