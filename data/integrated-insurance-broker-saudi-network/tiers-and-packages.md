## Broker versus insurer — the distinction that matters

| | **Insurer** | **Broker** |
|---|---|---|
| Underwrites risk | Yes | **No** |
| Issues the policy | Yes | No — the insurer does |
| Holds the provider network | **Yes** | **No** |
| Sets the class | **Yes** | **No** |
| Pays claims | Yes | No — the insurer does |
| Sells/advises | Sometimes | Yes |

Everything the advisor needs — the network and the class — belongs to the **insurer**, not the
broker.

## Why a broker name cannot be answered

If a member says "I'm with NIB":

- NIB cannot say which hospitals apply, because that depends on **which of its 20+ partners**
  underwrote the policy.
- NIB cannot state the class, because the insurer's policy schedule does.
- The member's **card** carries the insurer's name. That is the document to read.

So the correct agent response is **"which insurance company is on your card?"** — not a network
answer, and not a refusal.
