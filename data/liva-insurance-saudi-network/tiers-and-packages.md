Direct URL guesses for `/en/medical-insurance` and `/en/health-insurance` both 404.

## The mission statement mentions Medical — read it carefully

The About page's mission statement says the company provides products "in the areas of Property;
Marine; Motor; **Medical**; General Accident and Engineering."

That is the **licensed** scope inherited from Al Alamiya, not the **on-sale** product list. A licence
to underwrite a class is not the same as selling it, and the site offers no medical product page.

**Do not tell a user Liva sells health because the about page lists it.** The operative evidence is
the product menu and the sitemap: no medical line is purchasable.

## What Liva says about health — its own FAQ

> "We will continuously work on exploring new and innovative ways to expand our products and service
> offerings to include a full suite of life, **health**, personal, and commercial insurance products"

This is the closest Liva comes to health: a statement about **future** plans. It is not an offering.

Recording it matters because it is the one sentence a searcher could misread as "Liva does health."
The skill flags it explicitly.
